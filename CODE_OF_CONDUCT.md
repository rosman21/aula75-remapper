# Code of conduct

Be respectful. Assume good faith, give constructive feedback, and keep discussion about the
work, not the person. Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language aren't welcome.

The maintainer may edit, close, or remove contributions and block users who break these
rules. Report problems privately through
[GitHub's reporting tools](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) or by contacting the
maintainer, [@rosman21](https://github.com/rosman21).

This is a short statement in the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
